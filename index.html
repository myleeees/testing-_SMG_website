About SMG

Built to Bridge Design and Delivery

SMG Facade is an Australian-owned facade management and technical coordination company based in New South wales.

Founded to provide a smarter and more coordinated approach to facade delivery, we bridge the gap between design, procurement, compliance, and construction.

Our team combines technical expertise with practical project experience to support developers, builders, architects, consultants, suppliers, and installation teams across Australia.
